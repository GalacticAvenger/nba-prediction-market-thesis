- The original `PREDICTIONS.md` describes selection-free game-clock windows and lists paired `price − P*` as the primary localization measure, with outcome-based `Y − price` checks.
- Later `DEVIATIONS.md` notes describe the equal-game final-five quiet-stoppage `price − payoff` estimate as the primary outcome estimate and document changes to estimator labeling.
- The author is not able to verify from a dated protocol that the exact outcome estimator and the implemented timeout/restart filters were fixed before results were examined.
- The thesis therefore calls the quiet-stoppage analysis planned in the project notes, discloses the uncertainty, and does not claim independently verified preregistration or confirmatory status.
- The final-five weighting comparison and retrospective realized-end selection model are presented with their actual analytical status and limitations.

This record does not resolve intent or establish that any analysis was selected improperly. It makes the surviving documentation and its limits clear to readers.
